🌿 Using fresh spinach instead? Substitution guide — not part of the original recipe
  1. Use about 1.5–1.75 lb fresh spinach to replace the 20 oz frozen called for — fresh cooks down a lot.
  2. Wilt it in batches in a hot pan or by pouring boiling water over it, just until fully collapsed.
  3. Let it cool enough to handle comfortably.
  4. Squeeze it in a clean towel or cheesecloth — press hard, this is the step people skip.
  5. It's dry enough when almost no liquid drips out and the ball feels dense, not spongy.
  6. Chop it the same way you would the thawed frozen spinach, then use it in the same steps.

Dip's Ready

Let it sit for a couple of minutes before serving — it's molten. Serve warm, straight from the baking dish.

Serving Suggestion

Warm tortilla chips are the classic pairing for a dip like this — not part of the original recipe, just a serving idea. Toasted baguette or crackers work too.

Storage & Reheating

Cool, then refrigerate airtight up to 3–4 days. Reheat covered in a 325°F oven until warmed through, or in short microwave bursts, stirring between each. Add a splash of cream if it's tightened up.
